Pedagogical Framework

The Four Beats Rule

Every lesson, when eventually taught, runs four beats in this order:

BEAT 01 What Broke What broke before it existed, and why previous tools failed.
BEAT 02 The Fix The precise fix, with year, people, and engineering context.
BEAT 03 What It Cost The tradeoffs created, because every fix creates the next problem.
BEAT 04 Interview Question The exact question an interviewer asks about this concept.
Curriculum Lesson Stories: Every task has a dedicated historical story and Four Beats analysis.
